# Business Health Dashboard for New Wheels

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2e614ced-efd0-479d-a633-2888c62ed32d)

1. Project Objectives

* Create a quarterly business report to assess the health of the company using key business metrics
* Identify trends in revenue, orders, customer satisfaction, and shipping times to support strategic decisions
* Answer business-critical questions to provide insights for growth and improvement
  
2. Key Highlights

* Analyzed total revenue, total orders, total customers, and average ratings to summarize business performance
* Explored customer feedback to identify percentage distributions of good vs. bad feedback and trends over time
* Found insights on top vehicle makers by customer preference, as well as most preferred vehicle maker per state
* Identified quarter-over-quarter revenue growth and trends in orders and shipping times
* Calculated the average discount offered based on credit card type
* Analyzed customer satisfaction over time and provided actionable insights on customer trends
